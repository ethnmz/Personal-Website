daily apps

daily apps

the apps i open every day and what i actually use them for. mostly productivity stuff.

todoist

[tasks] [paid monthly] [since high school]

My to-do list for literally everything: school, home, and personal stuff. School is the number one thing I use it for. I’ve been using it since high school and pay for the monthly subscription because I love how I can organize everything.

beeper

[messaging] [macbook + iphone]

If you haven’t heard of it, Beeper connects all your other messaging apps into one app. I use it on my MacBook and iPhone so I don’t need the social media apps on my phone and can still message everybody.

copilot money

[finances]

How I manage my finances. I like that I can connect all my credit cards, bank accounts, and investing accounts in one place.

obsidian

[notes] [drawing tablet]

My note-taking app. Sometimes I pair it with a drawing tablet to take notes in my classes. I like it because everything is stored locally, and you can really customize it and use it however you want.

notion

[personal] [club work] [calendar + events]

I use Notion for personal stuff, club work, and keeping track of my calendars and all my events.
