AI assistants and search

Instead of ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro for everyday use

  • Ollama
    MIT★ 184kMac, Windows, Linux

    Runs open models such as Gemma, Qwen, Llama and gpt-oss on your own machine with one command.

  • Jan
    Apache-2.0★ 45kMac, Windows, Linux

    A desktop chat app for local models, with optional API keys. Easiest place to start if you've never used a terminal.

  • Open WebUI
    Open WebUI License★ 156kself-hosted

    A ChatGPT-style interface with document chat, web search and multiple users.

Instead of Perplexity

  • Vane, formerly Perplexica
    MIT★ 37kself-hosted

    An answer engine with cited sources. Works with local models or your own key.

  • SearXNG
    AGPL-3.0★ 39kself-hosted

    The private metasearch engine that most open answer engines use underneath.

  • Morphic
    Apache-2.0★ 9.2kweb or self-hosted

    A lighter answer engine with a generative interface.

Instead of Perplexity Comet and other AI browsers

  • BrowserOS
    AGPL-3.0★ 14kMac, Windows, Linux

    A Chromium-based browser with a built-in agent that can browse for you.

  • Nanobrowser
    Apache-2.0★ 14kChrome extension

    Web automation agents inside the browser you already use.

Instead of Cluely

  • Pluely
    GPL-3.0★ 2.7kMac, Windows, Linux

    A lightweight on-screen assistant for meetings and notes, with your own key.

Dictation and voice

Instead of Wispr Flow

  • Handy
    MIT★ 34kMac, Windows, Linux

    Push-to-talk dictation into any app. Speech recognition runs entirely on your computer.

  • OpenWhispr
    MIT★ 9.5kMac, Windows, Linux

    Local Whisper dictation, with optional cloud cleanup using your own key.

  • whisper.cpp
    MIT★ 54k

    The speech engine that most of these apps are built on.

Instead of ElevenLabs

  • Voicebox
    MIT★ 58kMac, Windows, Linux

    Local voice cloning, text-to-speech and dictation.

  • Chatterbox
    MIT★ 27kPython

    A high-quality open text-to-speech model with emotion control.

Studying

Instead of Turbo AI and other AI note apps

  • Open Notebook
    MIT★ 40kself-hosted

    A NotebookLM-style tool that turns PDFs and videos into notes, summaries and audio overviews. Works with local models or your own key.

  • Anki
    AGPL-3.0★ 32kMac, Windows, Linux, Android

    The spaced-repetition flashcard app students have used since 2006.

Video, images and slides

Instead of CapCut

  • OpenCut
    MIT★ 95kweb and desktop

    A CapCut-style editor.

  • Shotcut
    GPL-3.0★ 15kMac, Windows, Linux

    A mature timeline editor with more than 15 years of releases.

  • Kdenlive
    GPL-3.0Mac, Windows, Linux

    A full-featured editor from the KDE community.

  • Open Video Editor
    GPL-3.0Android

    Simple trimming and filters on your phone.

Instead of OpusClip

  • FunClip
    MIT★ 6.4kPython

    Finds and cuts the key moments from long videos using local speech recognition. Vertical reframing is still manual.

Instead of Higgsfield and other AI image or video generators

  • ComfyUI
    GPL-3.0★ 138kMac, Windows, Linux

    Node-based image and video generation with open models on your own GPU.

  • InvokeAI
    Apache-2.0★ 29kMac, Windows, Linux

    A friendlier canvas-style studio for local image models.

Instead of Gamma

  • Presenton
    Apache-2.0★ 11kdesktop or Docker

    Generates editable decks from a prompt and exports PowerPoint and PDF.

  • Slidev
    MIT★ 49k

    Slides written in Markdown.

Health

Instead of Flo

  • drip.
    GPL-3.0iOS and Android, hosted on GitLab

    A privacy-first cycle tracker with no account and no cloud. Available in both app stores.

Instead of Cal AI

  • OpenNutriTracker
    GPL-3.0★ 2.7kiOS and Android

    Calorie and macro tracking with barcode scanning and the Open Food Facts database. There is no mature open-source photo-based calorie app yet.

Utilities

    MIT★ 7kMac

    Menu-bar sleep prevention, maintained since 2014.

    MITMac

    Keeps a Mac running with the lid closed.

  • Microsoft PowerToys
    MIT★ 139kWindows

Installing and updating apps

  • Homebrew
    BSD-2-ClauseMac and Linux

    Installs most Mac apps above with one command.

  • Obtainium
    GPL-3.0★ 21kAndroid

    Installs and updates Android apps straight from their GitHub releases.

Nothing matches that. Try the name of the app you pay for.

Supporting these projects

If one of these saves you a subscription, the best way to say thanks is to give directly. Most of these repositories have a Sponsor button or an Open Collective link at the top of their GitHub page, and that money goes to the maintainers.
